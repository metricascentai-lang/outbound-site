How it works

From a product name to a booked conversation.

Every buyer moves through the same five steps, and each one is shown on screen with its source, so your team can check the work before anything goes out.

  1. Find

    Searches the web and company databases, and looks for companies like the customers you already have.

  2. Prove the fit

    Reads each company's own pages and scores it. Only companies with real evidence are kept, with the quote and the link.

  3. Name the person

    The right role at each company, such as purchasing, the owner or R&D, with the switchboard and a checked email.

  4. Write the message

    An email that opens with a line about the buyer's own products, or a call script where the rules say call first.

  5. Track it

    A shared pipeline from found to customer. Bring in your own contact lists too.
